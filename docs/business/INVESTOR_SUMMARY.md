## 🌟 About Fixate

Fixate is revolutionizing the device repair industry in Saudi Arabia by creating a
seamless bridge between customers and certified repair technicians. Customers spend
less time hunting for trustworthy technicians and stand-by repair slots; technicians
spend less time finding leads. The platform handles location matching, dispatch,
real-time tracking, transparent pricing, and payment.

### The problem we solve
- Long waiting times at traditional repair shops
- Lack of price transparency
- Difficulty finding trustworthy technicians
- No convenient way to request on-site repairs

### Our solution
- **Instant booking** — request a repair in under 60 seconds
- **Transparent pricing** — final cost agreed before work starts
- **Verified technicians** — Saudi National-ID + IBAN + document review
- **Location-based matching** — find the closest available technician
- **Real-time tracking** — live map of the technician en route

## 🎯 Service options

### Mobile technician
On-site repair. Technician travels to the customer with a fully equipped van.
- Best for: screen, battery, charging port, audio
- Time: same day or scheduled slot

### Pickup & delivery
Workshop repair. A courier picks up the device, the workshop performs the repair,
and the courier returns it.
- Best for: motherboard, processor, water damage, micro-soldering
- Time: 1–3 days
